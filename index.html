How we work

Coaching, not veterinary care

Calveroo sells no birds, no feed and no equipment, and takes no commission from anyone who does. The advice can therefore be to build rather than buy, or to keep three hens instead of eight.

We're also not veterinarians. A sick or injured bird needs a vet who treats poultry, and we'll help you find one and describe the symptoms clearly rather than guessing at medication. Local ordinances are yours to check — they vary enormously, sometimes street by street.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

How many chickens should I start with?

Three or four. They're flock birds so never one, and small numbers are much easier while you learn.

Do I need a rooster for eggs?

No. Hens lay without one, and most cities ban roosters anyway. You need a rooster only for fertilized eggs.

Is chicken wire enough?

No, and it's the most common mistake we see. Chicken wire keeps chickens in; hardware cloth keeps predators out.

My hen looks unwell. What do I do?

Isolate her, keep her warm and hydrated, and call a vet who sees poultry. We can help you describe what you're seeing — we don't diagnose or prescribe.

How long do hens lay?

Best production is the first two or three years, then it declines while they can live much longer. Worth deciding how you feel about that before starting.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.
