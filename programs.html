The programs, in detail

Four programs of 8 to 12 weeks, planned for your yard, your climate and your local rules.

Program 01 · Before you buy chicks

Your first flock

We start with your city or HOA rules, because people buy chicks and then discover roosters are banned. Then breed choice for your climate and temperament, realistic flock size, brooder setup, and an honest account of the first two months — which are the demanding ones.

  • 8 weeks
  • Before your birds arrive
  • 3 hours a week
  • A brooder space

Program 02 · Built once, properly

Coop & predators

Most coop problems are ventilation and security. We size the coop and run properly, fix airflow without drafts, and go through predator-proofing in detail — hardware cloth rather than chicken wire, buried aprons, latches raccoons can't work, and covered runs.

  • 8 weeks
  • Any keeper
  • 3 hours a week
  • Photos of your setup

Start this program

Program 03 · Notice it early

Health & behavior

Chickens hide illness, so the skill is knowing what normal looks like in your own birds. We cover the routine ten-minute watch, checking for mites, recognizing respiratory and crop problems, pecking and bullying, and broody hens — and we refer to a veterinarian rather than prescribing anything.

  • 10 weeks
  • Have birds
  • 2 hours a week
  • A flashlight

Program 04 · The whole year

Seasons & laying

Egg production falls in molt and in short days, and that's normal rather than a problem to fix. We work through the year: molt support, winter without frostbite, summer heat management, and honest expectations about how long hens lay well.
